How LifeProof Works

From university admission to dream career placement in four transparent steps.

01

Verified Onboarding

Students, faculty, and recruiters join with authenticated credentials. Student academic records are tied directly to university verification.

02

Skill Mapping

AI algorithms evaluate coursework, personal projects, and technical tests to generate an authenticated Career Readiness Score.

03

Recruiter Match

Enterprise recruiters search and filter candidates by verified competencies, initiating direct interviews and campus drives.

04

Placement & Sync

Offers and verification certificates are automatically recorded. Faculty and administration receive instantaneous placement data.
